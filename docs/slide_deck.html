The Clinical Hook

Why climate is dangerous to high-level SCI independent of devices.

  • 🌡️
    Autonomic dysfunction. SCI above T6 interrupts the autonomic nervous system's ability to thermoregulate.
  • 🥶
    Hyperthermia and hypothermia. The body cannot defend its core temperature even at mild ambient extremes.
  • ⚡
    Powered DME dependency. Ventilators, oxygen concentrators, powered wheelchairs, suction, IV infusion fail when the grid fails.
  • 🌪️
    Climate-driven outages. Extreme weather is the modern primary driver of large-scale grid disruption — and current frameworks do not address this convergence.
Methodology

A dual-percentile convergent-risk index.

Equal-weighted mean of national percentile ranks of two independent axes — chosen so neither raw scale dominates the index.

1

Hazard score

FEMA NRI 5-hazard composite: hurricane, heat wave, wildfire, coastal flood, drought.

2

Power-dep rate

HHS emPOWER electricity-dependent Medicare benes ÷ total Medicare benes × 100.

3

Percentile rank

National percentile rank of each axis. Median power-dep ≈ 5%; median hazard ≈ 50.

4

Dual-percentile VI

Equal-weighted mean of the two ranks. Top of index = both axes simultaneously elevated.

5

Spatial pipeline

Spearman ρ, Moran's I, LISA, attributable burden, spatial-error regression.

Where Convergent Risk Concentrates

Two distinct structural typologies.

Typology A

Intermountain drought-and-dependency corridor

Concentrated electricity-dependent populations paired with regional drought, wildfire, and heat-wave hazard. Dominates the national top-25.

  • San Juan NM, El Paso CO, Bernalillo NM
  • Sandoval NM, Adams CO, Mesa CO
  • Pueblo CO, Iron UT, Millard UT, Garfield UT
Typology B

Southeast hurricane-and-coastal-flood coast

Top-1% FEMA hazard percentiles paired with the largest absolute electricity-dependent counts at that hazard band. Includes the grant-holder's own service area.

  • Palm Beach FL (hazard 99.8) — top 1%
  • Miami-Dade FL (hazard 99.7) — 20,443 benes
  • Hillsborough FL, Broward FL

Limitations & Caveats

What this map does not measure.

  • 📍
    Cross-sectional only. Maps where convergent risk is structurally located — not behavioral adaptation, recovery, or coping. Those constructs require event-time data and are reserved for Phase 2.
  • 🧬
    SCI is not isolatable. Operational denominator is electricity-dependent Medicare beneficiaries (HHS emPOWER); SCI-specific identification requires CMS ResDAC DUA — out of scope for Part 1.
  • 🧓
    Medicare undercount. Working-age people with disabilities not on Medicare are excluded from the denominator. The 24-month SSDI waiting period further narrows the cohort.
  • 🇵🇷
    Puerto Rico hazard gap. FEMA NRI returns Insufficient Data for all 78 PR municipios — shown on the power-dependency axis only.
Takeaway

One map. Two pathways. A clinical-grade case for disability-inclusive preparedness.

Counties in the top vulnerability quartile — and PR municipios on the absolute-count axis — should be prioritized for preparedness protocols designed around the survival-critical electricity dependency the data actually measures, not generic "vulnerable population" framings.
